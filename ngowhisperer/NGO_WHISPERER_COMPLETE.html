The NGO Whisperer®

Global Fellowship Programme 2026

An accredited seven-month rigorous virtual leadership programme for exceptional leaders and founders of non-governmental organisations (NGOs) and social enterprises worldwide.

Objectives of Global Fellowship Programme

The NGO Whisperer® Global Fellowship Programme is designed to build capacity and create a global community of leaders focused on high-impact programmes.

Build Capacity

To build capacity of professionals from small and medium-sized NGOs and Social Enterprises who are already in positions of leadership and influence to further enhance and expand their knowledge, skills, and expertise.

Provide Training

To provide high-impact training, professional development, and cross-cultural opportunities for NGOs and Social Enterprises across the globe especially in low- and middle-income countries.

Create Community

To create a global community of leaders, influencers, and decision-makers, who are focused on designing and implementing high-impact programmes that contribute to building a sustainable world.
