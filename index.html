Who We Are

Welcome to
Spirit Embassy Kisumu

We are a vibrant community of believers, passionate about spreading the Good News of Jesus Christ in Kisumu and beyond. At Spirit Embassy, we believe in the transformative power of God's Word and the Holy Spirit.

Under the anointed leadership of our ministers, every service is a life-changing encounter with God's presence. Whether you are new to the faith or a seasoned believer, there is a place for you here.

Come experience dynamic worship, powerful teachings, and a community that genuinely cares about your spiritual growth and well-being.
